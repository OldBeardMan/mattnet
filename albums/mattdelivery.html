LET ME DELIVER SOME DELETED TRACKS TO YOU

Matt Delivery is super rare matt krupa release of deleted tracks from previous albums. It is the biggest one yet (CD release contains 22 songs!)

Microprocessor is a track that I composed while waiting for an exam on my studies. I didn't learn anything, it was impossibly difficult and I still passed the test by pure luck. It reflects my past two years, which were, by far, the best in my life.

All of the photos in the cover art are made with my old Nokia 515 that I have been using for 8 years, which I had to change now, because it was no longer possible to receive calls lmao. Photos taken during 2024-2025.
