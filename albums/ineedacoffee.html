I Need A Coffee

The first single from "Zzz"

This is a first single from an upcoming album called "Zzz".

After the lore of Matt Krupa ended in "Light Of Spring" 4 months ago I decided to collect some of the more ambient tracks deleted from the previous album and make a few more. I couldn't decide at what directrion to go with the theme of the album, but recently when I realized how important good sleep is and how many thoughts I get after sleeping (or trying to sleep...)

The first track that I'm releasing (AT THE FIRST DAY OF AUTUMNT HEPPY AUTISM) to the public is this: I Need A Coffee. Big mood name. After years of drinking this magical potion I got immune to it so drinking it just makes me heart go faster, but still sleepy.

Released September 23, 2026. Available on Bandcamp only for now, and as a music video below.
