Pls Come

The one that stayed after the Winter

The only song from "Winter (Presents)" that is still out there.

'Pls Come' here you have a christamss song! Or more like Advent song?? idk, but it's the only vocal one on the album. I wrote it about Christ coming to Earth in both Christmas and Parousia.

It was first released on Christmas Eve 2021, together with the whole "Winter (Presents)" album - a record where every song was a present for someone from my family.

Later I took the album down from streaming and Bandcamp, so the only way to hear it now is the CD released in December 2021. 'Pls Come' is the one exception: it went back up on Bandcamp as a single, so it is still out there for everyone.

Released December 24, 2021. Available on Bandcamp, on the "Winter (Presents)" CD, and as a music video below.
