The story

Afmeli wakes up on a forest floor next to a small cabin, with no idea how she got there. A big round cat named Sprytek greets her. He says this forest was made from his owner's music, and that she disappeared after writing "one more song". The kittens born from her notes are now scattered across the woods, and they need someone to bring them home.

Explore a handcrafted voxel world through the seasons, from a red-and-gold autumn forest to snowed-in winter woods and further on. Find the lost kittens one by one and carry them back to the cabin. Read the notes left behind. Keep yourself awake with coffee, because something drifts along the paths at night. Don't listen to the Whispers.

  • Hand-drawn pixel art sprites in a first-person voxel world
